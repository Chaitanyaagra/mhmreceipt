About the Society

A registered welfare society, run in the open.

Max Heights Majestic Resident Welfare Society manages the upkeep, security and shared finances of our community. This portal is how that work stays visible to every member — who's registered, what's been collected, and what every rupee was for.

1 REGISTER

Submit your details once

Fill the resident form with your flat, tower and contact details. The committee reviews and approves it.

2 PAY

Choose how you pay

Cash or cheque at the RWA office, or UPI and bank transfer from your phone. Both are tracked identically.

3 VERIFY

Get a sealed receipt

Once the Treasurer verifies your payment, a numbered receipt with a scannable QR seal is issued to you.

Privacy Policy

What data we collect: Name, mobile, email, photo, Date of Birth, Anniversary, family members' names/relation/DOB, and your payment history.

Why we collect it: To verify membership, generate maintenance bills, provide a photo ID for gate/security, and send birthday/anniversary wishes.

Who can see it: Only the society's committee members (who log in to the admin panel) — it is never shown publicly. Your own data is visible only to you and the committee.

How long we keep it: For as long as you're a resident of the society. Financial records (payment receipts) are kept longer for audit purposes, as required by law.

Your rights: You can view/edit your own data from "My Profile". When you sell or vacate your flat, you can have the committee erase your personal data (mobile, email, family details, photo, ID) — only your name, flat number, and financial history (needed for audit) are retained.

Questions? Please contact the society office.
