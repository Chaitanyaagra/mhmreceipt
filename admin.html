Payment Settings (shown to residents)

Maintenance Rate

This rate determines how much each flat owes. Without it, the system has no way of knowing who has paid in full and who's paid half — and the Defaulters report stays empty.

Applies to all towers unless overridden below.
Tower-wise alag rate (optional)
A one-time joining fee charged to every new member. Defaults to ₹1100 if left blank.

Document Security

Access to Aadhaar/PAN uploads is always restricted to Admins by Storage rules. This adds an optional AES-256 encryption layer on top — see SETUP-GUIDE.md for the key-management trade-off before relying on this.

Backup

This downloads a JSON file to your computer — no Drive setup needed. Take one once a month and keep it somewhere safe.

Google Drive Backup

Exports Members, Payments, Notices & Committee as JSON to MHMRWS/Database in your Google Drive. Requires one-time OAuth setup — see SETUP-GUIDE.md Step 5.

Data Health Check

Firestore has no foreign-key enforcement — nothing stops a payment, pet, or complaint from quietly pointing at a member record that no longer exists (most often after a manual data-erase or an old member being cleaned up by hand). This scans for exactly that. It only reports what it finds — nothing here is changed or deleted automatically, since the right fix for each one depends on why it happened.

Privacy Policy

What data we collect: Name, mobile, email, photo, Date of Birth, Anniversary, family members' names/relation/DOB, and payment history.

Why we collect it: To verify membership, generate maintenance bills, provide a photo ID for gate/security, and send birthday/anniversary wishes.

Who can see it: Only the society's committee members (admin panel login) — it is never shown publicly.

How long we keep it: For as long as the resident is with the society. Financial records are kept longer for audit purposes, as required by law.

Resident's rights: They can view/edit their own data from "My Profile". When they sell or vacate their flat, the committee can erase their personal data (mobile, email, family details, photo, ID) — only their name, flat number, and financial history are retained.
